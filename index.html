Why Whoof

You shouldn’t feel anxious about your pet when you travel.

We’re pet parents too. We know the guilt of leaving them behind, and the worry of leaving them with someone you barely know.

That’s why we’re building Whoof: a selective, personal pet-sitting platform where you get to know your sitter before you leave your pet in their care.

For now, all the sitting is done by us, the founders. Whether it’s a few hours during the day or a few nights away, we keep it personal and get to know every pet and parent ourselves.

Good questions.

Can’t find what you’re looking for? Message us and we’ll happily answer.

How much does it cost?

It depends on how long your pet needs sitting, and we can discuss it after our first call. The meet & greet is always free.

Can I leave my pet just for the day?

Of course. Whether it’s a few hours while you’re at work or out for the day, or a few nights away, we’re happy to help.

Which pets can stay with you?

We welcome friendly dogs and cats, big or small. If your pet is reactive, very young, elderly or needs medication, tell us when we talk and we’ll work out whether we’re the right fit.

Do you sit cats too?

Yes! We welcome both dogs and cats.

Do pets need to be vaccinated?

Yes. We ask that pets are up to date on core vaccinations (including rabies) and on tick & flea prevention, to keep every guest safe.

What happens in an emergency?

Before the stay, we note your vet’s details and an emergency contact. If anything comes up, we call you straight away and take your pet to the nearest vet if needed.

What should I pack?

Their usual food (sudden switches upset tummies), any medication, a leash, and something that smells like home: a favourite toy, blanket or bed.

After hearing so many pet parents share the same struggle, we decided to do something about it. For now, it’s just the two of us doing all the sitting: your pet stays in a home, with people who love having them around.

We’re so excited to meet you (and your pet, of course)!
